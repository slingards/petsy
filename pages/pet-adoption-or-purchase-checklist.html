Pet Adoption or Purchase Checklist: Questions to Ask Before Committing

Taking time to research an animal before committing can prevent a mismatch between what you expect and what the animal actually needs.

The most useful questions are not only about price or appearance. You want enough information to understand the animal's current condition, routine and future care requirements.

Understand the animal's background

Ask what is known about age, diet, daily routine, behavior, previous housing and any relevant medical history. Written records are useful because they reduce the chance of important details being forgotten.

Ask about current care

Find out what the animal eats, how often it is fed, what enrichment it receives, how it handles grooming and what its normal sleeping pattern looks like. Matching the first days at home to the existing routine can make the transition smoother.

Review health information

Request available veterinary records, vaccination information and parasite-treatment history where applicable. If something is unclear, ask a veterinarian to help interpret the information before making a long-term decision.

Think about behavior realistically

Ask how the animal responds to handling, unfamiliar people, other animals, noise and routine changes. No short description can predict every future behavior, but honest background information helps you prepare.

Calculate the real cost

Estimate food, housing, routine healthcare, grooming, enrichment, training and emergency expenses. A lower initial cost does not necessarily mean lower lifetime cost.

Check your own readiness

Compare the animal's needs with your schedule, housing and experience. If the species requires specialized care, make sure you have access to the appropriate professionals and equipment before committing.

Good research is specific: Replace “Is this a good pet?” with questions such as “How much daily exercise does this animal need?”, “What does its adult size mean for my home?” and “What recurring care will I need to budget for?”
