Legal, Conservation & Welfare Notice

Primates are highly intelligent social wildlife and are not ordinary household pets. Any request may require proof of legal eligibility, appropriate social housing, specialist facilities, trained staff, veterinary support, permits and complete documentation.

Great ape profiles and selected endangered primate profiles use an institutional or conservation enquiry instead of the ordinary request-cart flow.
