Reptile Welfare & Ownership Notice

Reptiles require species-specific care, secure enclosures, correct temperatures, humidity control, suitable lighting, clean water, an appropriate diet, safe handling and qualified exotic-animal veterinary support.

Some snakes, lizards, turtles and tortoises may be regulated or require permits. Confirm local ownership, transport, import, export and welfare requirements before starting a request.
