Cat Welfare & Ownership Notice

Cats and kittens require daily care, clean food and water, safe housing, litter care, social attention, proper handling and qualified veterinary support. Review each breed's temperament, grooming needs, activity level and long-term care requirements before starting a request.

Ask for available health and vaccination records, confirm current availability and make sure your home, schedule and budget are suitable for the cat you are considering.
