Dog Welfare & Ownership Notice

Dogs and puppies require daily care, proper feeding, clean water, safe housing, exercise, training, social attention and qualified veterinary support. Review each breed's temperament, activity level, grooming needs and long-term care requirements before starting a request.

Ask for available health and vaccination records, confirm current availability and make sure your home, schedule and budget are suitable for the dog you are considering.
