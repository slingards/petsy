Bird Welfare & Ownership Notice

Birds require suitable housing, clean food and water, safe handling, social interaction, exercise, environmental enrichment and access to qualified avian veterinary care. Review each species' lifespan, noise level, diet and space needs before starting a request.

Some bird species may require ownership, transport, import or export documentation. Confirm all local welfare and legal requirements before completing a request.
